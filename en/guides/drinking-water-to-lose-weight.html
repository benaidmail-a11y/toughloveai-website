Drinking Water to Lose Weight: Does It Really Help?

Updated November 26, 2026 · ToughLove editorial team · about 5 min read

In short: Yes, a little. In one study, people who drank half a litre of water before each main meal lost about two kilos more in twelve weeks than those who didn’t. Water is no miracle cure, but it’s a simple habit that fills you up and replaces sugary drinks.

What the study shows

Middle-aged and older adults followed a reduced-calorie diet for twelve weeks. Half also drank 500 ml of water before each main meal. This group lost about two kilos more than the comparison group (Dennis et al., 2010). The explanation is plausible: water fills the stomach, you start the meal less ravenous and eat a bit less.

The bigger effect: what you stop drinking

Water often helps most when it replaces something. Half a litre of soda, iced tea or juice can quickly contain as much sugar as a slice of cake but barely fills you up. Swapping your daily soft drinks for water saves a lot without giving up anything filling.

Thirsty or hungry?

Sometimes you “fancy something” without knowing what. Sometimes it’s thirst, sometimes tiredness or boredom. A glass of water and a five-minute break show whether it’s real hunger. If it is, eat calmly: water doesn’t replace a meal.

How to drink more without thinking about it

  1. A glass before every meal. Fixed rule, fixed cue: that’s how it becomes a habit.
  2. Keep the bottle in sight. On your desk, in the car, next to the sofa. What you see, you drink.
  3. First thing in the morning. A glass of water before coffee.
  4. Flavour without sugar. Lemon, cucumber, mint or unsweetened tea.
  5. No soft drinks at home. What isn’t there, you don’t have to resist.

How much makes sense?

For healthy adults, the German Nutrition Society recommends about 1.5 litres of drinks per day, more in heat and during exercise. More isn’t automatically better. If you have heart or kidney disease or your doctor has set a fluid limit, stick to it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you lose weight if you drink a lot of water?

Water alone doesn’t melt away weight. In one study, however, people on a diet who drank 500 ml before each meal lost about two kilos more. Water helps most when it replaces sugary drinks.

When should you drink water to lose weight?

The easiest way is a large glass about half an hour before each main meal, plus drinking through the day when thirsty.

How much water should you drink a day?

About 1.5 litres of drinks per day is recommended for healthy adults, more in heat and during exercise. With heart or kidney disease, agree the amount with your doctor.
