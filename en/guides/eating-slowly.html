Eating Slowly to Lose Weight: What Studies Say

Updated November 26, 2026 · ToughLove editorial team · about 5 min read

In short: People who eat more slowly eat less on average without being hungrier afterwards, as a review of 22 studies shows. It’s one of the simplest weight loss habits: it costs nothing, bans nothing and works without any calorie counting.

What research shows

In an experiment, 30 women ate the same meal once quickly and once slowly, with small bites, thorough chewing and pauses. Eating slowly, they consumed about 579 instead of 646 kilocalories on average and still felt fuller (Andrade, Greene & Melanson, 2008).

A larger review of 22 studies reached the same result: eating more slowly reduces how much you eat. Participants weren’t hungrier, neither right after the meal nor hours later (Robinson et al., 2014).

Why pace makes such a difference

Fullness doesn’t arise in your mouth but from signals from your stomach and gut that reach the brain only gradually. Fast eaters are often finished before “I’m full” even arrives, and notice later that it was too much. Eating slowly gives these signals time. Mindful eating also makes a meal more enjoyable, and enjoyment helps satisfy you.

7 tricks for eating more slowly

  1. Put your cutlery down. After each bite, set down the fork, chew, swallow, then continue.
  2. Take smaller bites. A teaspoon instead of a tablespoon works wonders.
  3. Eat without screens. If you look at your phone, you eat faster and notice less how much.
  4. Sit down and use a plate. Not standing, not out of the packet.
  5. Have a glass of water. Sipping creates natural pauses.
  6. Pause halfway. Ask yourself: “How full am I right now, from 1 to 10?”
  7. Don’t come to the table starving. When you’re ravenous, you gulp. Regular meals make slow eating easier.

Making slow eating a habit

The hardest part isn’t knowing it but sticking with it. Start with one meal a day, ideally your calmest one. After a few weeks the new pace feels normal and becomes a habit that works without counting anything.

Frequently asked questions

Do you lose weight if you eat more slowly?

In studies, eating more slowly reduces how much people eat per meal without making them hungrier afterwards. As a habit, it can help with weight loss.

How long should a meal take?

There’s no fixed rule. Many experts suggest taking about 20 minutes for a main meal so that fullness signals can arrive.

How can I get used to eating slowly?

Start with one meal a day, put your cutlery down between bites and eat without screens. After a few weeks the new pace becomes a habit.
