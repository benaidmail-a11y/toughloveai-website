Why ToughLove exists

For Sara. And for everyone whose problem isn’t knowledge.

My friend Sara has been trying to lose weight for as long as I’ve known her. She knows exactly what’s healthy. She has done diets, counted calories, tried apps. It’s never a lack of knowledge. It’s always the same moment: in the evening, when the craving comes – and no one is there to say: “Wait a second. What’s really going on?”

No app we knew was there in that moment. They count, they remind, they praise. But when it gets hard, you’re on your own.

So I built ToughLove: a coach who is honest when you’re fooling yourself – and there when it counts.

FAQ

Good to know.

How much does ToughLove cost?

There will be a free version. Premium is planned at €9.99 per month or €59.99 per year, with 7 days free to try it. The subscription is coming soon.

Does the app count calories?

No, on purpose. ToughLove works on your habits and moments, not on numbers.

Is the coach a human?

No. Your coach is an AI with its own personality and memory – and says so if you ask.

Does ToughLove replace a doctor or dietitian?

No. ToughLove is a motivation and habit coach. For health problems or signs of an eating disorder, the app points you to professional help.

What happens to my data?

Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. So the coach can reply, messages are processed by Google Gemini – not used for training. No ads, no selling, and you can delete everything in the app at any time. Details in the privacy policy (German).

Is ToughLove available for iPhone?

Android only for now. An iPhone version is planned for later.
